Delete Your Account

MusicLessonRecap · Last updated: August 7, 2026

MusicLessonRecap is developed by Al Qy. This page explains how to request deletion of your MusicLessonRecap account and associated data.

How to request deletion

Send an email to oluqu56@gmail.com from the email address associated with your MusicLessonRecap account, with the subject line "Delete my account."

What happens next

  1. We will verify the request comes from the account owner.
  2. Your account, lesson notes, student records, and any saved repertoire data will be permanently deleted.
  3. If you have an active subscription, it will be canceled. Any billing history retained by our payment processor (Stripe) for tax and accounting purposes is not affected by this deletion and is governed by Stripe's own retention policies.
  4. Deletion is typically completed within 7 days of a verified request.

What data is deleted

  • Your account profile (name, email, password)
  • All student records you created
  • All lesson notes and history
  • Saved repertoire and preferences

What data may be retained

  • Billing records kept by Stripe as required by law for tax and accounting purposes

Questions

If you have questions about this process, contact oluqu56@gmail.com.
