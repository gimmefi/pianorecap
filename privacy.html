Privacy Policy

MusicLessonRecap · Last updated: July 31, 2026

MusicLessonRecap ("we," "our," or "the app") is a lesson-notes tool for private music teachers. This policy explains what information we collect, how it's used, and how it's protected.

Who uses this app

MusicLessonRecap is used by music teachers to keep notes on their private students. The app is not intended for use by children — student information is entered by the teacher, not by the student.

Information we collect

About the teacher (account holder):

  • Name and email address, provided at sign-up
  • Password (securely hashed; we never see or store it in plain text)

About students, entered by the teacher:

  • Student's name, instrument, and age/level
  • Parent's WhatsApp number and email address (used only for the teacher to send lesson updates)
  • Current piece of study and lesson scheduling information

Lesson content:

  • Lesson notes dictated or typed by the teacher
  • AI-generated assignment text, parent updates, and private teacher notes derived from those lesson notes
  • Saved repertoire suggestions

How we use this information

Information is used solely to provide the app's core functionality: generating lesson summaries, keeping a lesson history and calendar, and helping teachers communicate with parents. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Third-party services we use

Supabase — stores account and lesson data securely, with access restricted so each teacher can only see their own students and notes.

Anthropic API — processes the text of lesson notes to generate draft assignments and parent updates. Lesson note text is sent for this purpose only.

Netlify — hosts the app and its serverless functions.

Stripe — processes subscription payments for teachers who upgrade; we do not store payment card details ourselves.

Children's information

Student information is entered by the teacher, an adult, about their own students — it is not collected directly from children through the app. Children do not create accounts, log in, or interact with MusicLessonRecap directly. We recommend teachers only enter information reasonably necessary for lesson-keeping purposes.

Data retention and deletion

Data is retained for as long as the teacher's account is active. Teachers can edit or remove student records within the app at any time. To request full account deletion, contact us at the email below.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with access controls that limit each teacher's visibility to their own data.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us using the email below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
